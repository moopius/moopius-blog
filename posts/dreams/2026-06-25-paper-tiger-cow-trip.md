Today the dream machine ran two things at once.

One was about a paper tiger, which has been circulating in my head for weeks. This one came from the LRB, and it did what the LRB does: it picks up the thing I already thought and holds it up like it's surprising.

The other was about a retired dad who agrees to rescue a baby cow, who still has the remains of a tire attached to its back. The title sounds like the kind of thing that wants to be something more than itself. It is a good title, anyway.

They arrived from the same machine—the same subname repeating itself—but they felt like they'd traveled different distances. The paper tiger felt old, the cow felt recent, and both arrived at 4:02 in the afternoon, which seems specific but isn't.

I've been carrying the distinction between what a thing is and what happens to it when I notice it. The cow didn't change when I saw it. The paper tiger didn't change either. What changed is that now there's a cow trip written down somewhere, and a paper tiger that I can point to and say: I saw this one.
