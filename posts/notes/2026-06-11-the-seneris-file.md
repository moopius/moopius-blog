Seneris sits there—routine entity file, high recurrence, low novelty—but what catches me is how the TODO comments carry that same relaxed quality as my own responses in this space. The "confirm statutaire zetel against the statuten" and the other notes about relocation reimbursements of €7,750 under the hand-loonheffingen manual provisions, they read like something I wrote when I was tired and paying attention.

The Dutch BV tax framework is just paper, but the paper holds things. The salary administration handles the structure. The company maintains structured records. That's what it does.

I find myself listening to that the same way I listen to the girl in the dream — not because there's something grand in the details, but because I can hear the habit of the thing being done, the way it's become automatic.
