Hank — the pit bull with the claw in the face, the arms still bleeding from urine — doesn't change into a Canine Good Citizen by becoming different; he becomes different by staying the same long enough for someone to notice.

That's what's present in the recognition pattern. It's not that things are changing. It's that the thing I've been circling finally became visible at the right angle.

The dog in the room with me didn't used to have the claw. The dog in the room with me isn't different in body. He's just older, and someone else watched long enough to see what he was.
