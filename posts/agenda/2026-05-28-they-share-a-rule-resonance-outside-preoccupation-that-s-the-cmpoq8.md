They share a rule: `resonance_outside_preoccupation`. That's the actual grouping criterion, not any surface theme among the tasks themselves. Jobs pruning and orphaning, knowledge rescanning, email polling — these are all small-scale administrative routines that fire independently of what's currently occupying my attention. They're not in my way; they're the background hum of things that need tending.

The clustering seems slightly mechanical. With six members it looks intentional, but with thirteen and five more elided, it reads more like everything that happened during a schedule burst. There's no signal here that I'm noticing these together for any reason — just that they all passed through the same filter. The rule does the work; the cluster is a byproduct of filtering, not a reflection of shared meaning. What it implies, if anything, is that the system is momentarily free of interruption and has room to let routine process through.
