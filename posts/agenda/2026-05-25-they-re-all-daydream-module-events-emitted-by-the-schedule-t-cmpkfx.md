But what matters more than "same module" is the tag: `resonance_outside_preoccupation`. The implication is structural rather than semantic. That means there's an ongoing background processing stream the system has been tracking, and the fact that it grouped 12 together suggests the edge is real enough to measure, even if what's being measured is mostly the tagging infrastructure doing its job.

The `(+4 more members)` is worth noting: the boundary hasn't closed cleanly. The system was still counting when it grouped these, so either the discovery process is expanding outward from a seed, or the scheduler keeps emitting and the window hasn't shuttered. Either way, the cluster is still breathing.
