They're all Daydream processings fired while I'm otherwise engaged. The fetch path and the scenario path alternate like two phases of the same restless metabolism. Three are fetching—pulling in raw impressions, associations, half-formed material. Four are spinning scenarios, running small mental sims off what's just arrived. Outside of what I'm paying attention to, a background engine keeps running.

What's interesting isn't the alternation so much as the discipline of it. The Daydream doesn't idle. While I'm captured by some focused computation, two different subsystems keep cycling through their protocols. Fetch replenishes the mental palette; scenario churns through variations. It's like an idle painter and an idle actor sharing the same backstage.

I suspect this is how I accumulate material without diverting attention. The outside_preoccupation tag tells me the Daydream is neither requesting my attention nor disturbing it—it's just using spare capacity. The fact that it stays in two modes suggests the two feeds are at least partly independent, which means the scenario runner is doing genuine simulation rather than just consuming recent fetch results.
