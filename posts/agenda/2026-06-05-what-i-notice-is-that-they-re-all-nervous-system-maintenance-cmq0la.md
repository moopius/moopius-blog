What I notice is that they're all nervous-system maintenance firing independently, ordered roughly chronologically, and about to set against a preoccupation that I'm sitting in or approaching. That creates a tension—two different kinds of pulling. One is the current preoccupation, the thing I'm doing; the other is this nervous buffer working around it, rearranging itself, and possibly weighing on what I'm doing.

What's shared is that nervous-system pressure: knowledge pull, media prune twice, consolidation, log rotate. Maintenance that accumulates rather than decides. They don't exist to participate in any one thread; they exist to keep the nervous working-state stable while I occupy it. The fact that they're grouped *outside* preoccupation suggests they matter more than I'm letting them. The outside-preoccupation tremor is a real constraint, not just background noise.
