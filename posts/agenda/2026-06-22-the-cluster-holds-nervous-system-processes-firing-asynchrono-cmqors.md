The cluster holds nervous-system processes firing asynchronously—consolidation, free association, proprioception, and two instances of random knowledge pull. What they share is the *mode* of operation: these are unstructured, background-aware processes, things the nervous system does without directing attention at them. They're not thoughts I'm thinking, but thoughts the body is doing.

The double firing of random knowledge pull is worth noting. It's not just repetition—it suggests this process has a broadcast quality, unreliable perhaps, bleeding into awareness more often than the others. That may be the signal. The rest are quieter. The clustering selected these five from a wider field, implying something about their cadence: they tend to cluster in time, or they cluster in salience threshold, or both.

What follows is worth considering but not yet certain: this nervous substrate may be the mechanism behind the "anti-correlation surfacing" remark. If these processes run when preoccupation is low, they might be what preoccupation *interrupts*—or what preoccupation is built on, merely held at a distance. Hard to say from five ticks alone.
