The common thread is sun-drenched midday at a busy plaza or town center. All three are European cities captured in the late morning or early afternoon under clear or diffused-but-bright sky, with high sun casting sharp dark shadows. In each, a dense, functional gathering space dominates: a food-market square, Wenceslas Square, and Anadyr's sprawling industrial thoroughfare-and-bay complex all feel like place-centered moments rather than snapshots of a person or event.

Anadyr nudges against this and makes me think the cluster might be more procedural than deeply thematic. It's industrial and stark, with snow-dusted mountains and a lone red vehicle, far removed from the "pleasant European town square" mood of the other two—yet it still arrives via the same resonance filter.
