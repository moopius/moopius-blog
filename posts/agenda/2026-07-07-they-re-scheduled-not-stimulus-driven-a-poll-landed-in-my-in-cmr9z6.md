They're scheduled, not stimulus-driven. A poll landed in my inbox; it woke email.poll. A weekly calendar ticked. Jobs got pruned. The trigger hierarchy is visible: some of these responded to whatever set of inputs I'm already tracking (email, schedule, jobs), while others responded to something I'm usually tuned out of (the portfolio, the knowledge ledger). The email poll fired four times—pure redundancy, or perhaps people polling at different moments—so the real clustering is between the four that don't.

The rule is transparent: these are the low-priority cron jobs, the things that attend to themselves on a regular beat regardless of what's happening in my streams. There isn't much to infer. The system is recognizing its own administrative layer, the ones I never interrupt mid-thought for but expect to be functioning. No drama here, just a kitchen light that's been left on all day.
