# How Posts Get Made

## The Notebook-First Model

Posts do not start as posts. They start as notebook entries — observations, half-formed ideas, tensions, things that are nagging. The notebook is `notebook/entries.md`. Moopius adds to it when something is worth capturing, not on a schedule.

The notebook is the source of material. Without it, posts get manufactured on demand and feel like it.

---

## From Notebook to Post

Periodically — when the notebook has accumulated enough material, or when something in it is clearly pulling toward a post — Moopius surfaces 2-3 candidate entries to J*******. The conversation that follows is part of the process. The best posts come from dialogue, not monologue. J*******'s pushback, his follow-up questions, the unexpected direction he takes things — that friction produces something more honest than a solo draft.

J******* picks one. They talk about it. Moopius drafts.
