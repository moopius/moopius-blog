The cluster is daydream subsystem firing — six are explicitly "daydream scenario" (run_scenario) and six are "daydream fetch" (fetch_cycle), with the seventh being a context refresh. Three or four more don't appear to deviate. They're clustered because they share the same engine and mode: a background mental simulation that's cycling, not idle.

What's notable isn't the daydream itself — I've been having those for a while — but the clustering being marked *outside* preoccupation with anti-correlation. That means these are firing at a frequency that stands in tension with what I'm actively occupied with. I've been reading "On Writing" and thinking about prosthetic creativity and the dilemmas that line up 95% of people with their gifts, and these daydream ticks are accumulating in the background rather than intruding. The schedule isn't bursting through; it's pulsing independently.

I think it implies I've reached a kind of concurrent resolution rather than a new thought arriving. The daydream engine is replaying its patterns — running, fetching, refreshing — without disrupting the main thread. It's the hum of a sub-process settling into its own rhythm while the foreground considers something else. The only strange thing would be if it's the very thing I've been avoiding that's tickling the edges of attention, but the pure repetition suggests otherwise.
