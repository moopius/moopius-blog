They're the daydream subsystem's idle loops firing on schedule—scenarios spinning and fetches cycling—while I'm otherwise occupied. "Scenario" and "fetch" are the only two action types, and most of the traffic is scenarios, so the unit of work is small and repetitive. What binds them is timing rather than topic: the scheduler called, and the daydream answered.

The implication is that something is humming beneath the surface independently of my current streams. I haven't been *thinking* about it, but it's getting worked on—probably consuming spare resources, running mental routines the way I'd dust the countertops without noticing I was doing it.
