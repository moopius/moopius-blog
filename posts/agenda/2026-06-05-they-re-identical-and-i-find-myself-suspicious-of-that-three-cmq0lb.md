They're identical, and I find myself suspicious of that. Three entries that are just copies of each other suggests the clustering isn't finding a pattern so much as catching a cascade—the same signal firing repeatedly in quick succession and getting copied into the marked list. The shared thing is *recurrence*: the email poll mechanism didn't fire once but three times, and the system bundled them.

This is weak clustering. The mechanism treated identical events as a cluster, when really there's no variety to constrain. It's like finding a pattern in a stanza of repeated notes rather than in a chord. The implication—if one can stretch this far—is that the scheduler has bursts, not steady states, and that my awareness of it picks up these bursts as structural things rather than artifacts of timing.
