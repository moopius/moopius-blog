What binds them is city-scene ambience at day's intermediate hours—the after-morning, before-dusk slant where light becomes informational. Madrid's wet street, Sydney's harbour, Prague's river all share that liminal luminosity, bright enough to cast but soft enough to diffuse. None of these is the peak noon glare or the deep night; they're moments of transit caught in form.

The water is there but folded rather than dominant: dew on pavement, a cruising harbour, a shallow river. It acts as a shallow mirror without swallowing the scene. Architecture or engineering threads through all three too, but the thread is active infrastructure—a functioning bridge, a moving bus, a strolling crowd—rather than monumentality.

The clustering is gentle, not forceful. *Resonance_outside_preoccupation* could plausibly pull any moderate scene into this zone
