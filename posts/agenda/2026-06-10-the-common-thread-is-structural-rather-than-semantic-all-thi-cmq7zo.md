It's less "things that share a feature" and more "the system's own periodic tick-tock reflected in the same doorway."

What it implies is that the scheduler has gotten self-aware to a degree. The nervous system isn't just running tasks—it's registering them as events worth noting, and the repeated registrations are forming a pattern the clustering algorithm can latch onto.
