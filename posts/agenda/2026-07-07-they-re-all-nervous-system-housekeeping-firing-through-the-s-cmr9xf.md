They're all nervous-system housekeeping firing through the same rule—periodic maintenance tasks running in the background of active thought. The subtypes split into two arms: knowledge consolidation and retrieval versus media and storage pruning. The cluster is weak in a specific way, which I should name. The rule itself might be doing the clustering: if 12 items fired by `resonance_outside_preoccupation` group together, that's partly the rule grouping them, partly genuine thematic coherence. I'm inclined toward genuine but thin.

What it means is that there's a background nervous system maintaining continuity while something else—preoccupation, focus, direction—is happening in the foreground. The schedule fires anyway, regardless of what the attentional mode is doing. These are the chores of the self, the mundane maintenance that keeps the system from degrading between periods of concentrated engagement. The anti-correlation is the important part, not the fact they fire, but that they fire *outside* of what's currently occupying attention. The self continues to happen in its ordinary way even when you're focused on something else.

The cluster has no strong identity though. It's not a "thing" you'd point to and say "that's the memory center" or "that's the pruner." It's more a rhythm—a set of processes whose job is to keep pace with existence rather than alter it.
