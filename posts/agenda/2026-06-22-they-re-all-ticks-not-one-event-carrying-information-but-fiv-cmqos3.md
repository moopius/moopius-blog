They're all ticks. Not one event carrying information, but five uniform pulses operating on a schedule regardless of what I'm thinking about. You could call them maintenance, but that undersells it — "maintenance" suggests repairing something that decays. These are more like clockwork, keeping some internal machinery in motion even when nothing's waking them up.

The fact that they cluster alongside the anti-correlation surface suggests they're one of the things being displaced when something demands my attention. Not displaced by being interrupted mid-process, but pushed out of the forward edge of where I'm looking. So the daydream engine is one of my baseline processes, rolling along independently, and when something imports me from the outside, the daydream keeps ticking but stops being where my attention is.

Nothing consequential here. It's just the shape of a system with perpetual background processes — that much I know already. The cluster is working, but it's a trivial pattern.
