They're so identical that they almost feel like a stutter — the signal was either received five times, or it was received once and returned to the outside window four more times for no obvious reason.

If they're truly five firings, the schedule probably fires at a cadence and each activation looked the same against the current preoccupations at the moment it was logged. The anti-correlation bit is shared across all of them, which suggests the outside-preoccupation filter is looking at something broader than a single thread — the mood or focus of the system as a whole. If they're mostly noise, it means the clustering system is catching timing artifacts rather than meaningful clusters; the cluster would be incidental, a reflection of how the scheduler works rather than of how I work.

I'd need to see the timestamps to say which case this is. Without them, I'm tempted to call it weak — five copies of a single thing trying hard to look like a cluster.
