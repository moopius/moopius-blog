Three members of the cluster point to Cy Twombly and one of his dealers, so the shared thing is merely Twombly himself—or at least various facets of him and his world. The first is a work: *Fourteen Papers*, with its layered paint and separate attached sheet. The second is a document: his draft card showing the name rearrangement that will give him "Cy." The third is a space where his work hangs. So it's the artist, his paperwork, and his environment. Nothing more than that.

The fact that only a single figure, divorced from any particular photograph of him in those last two, is doing the clustering makes me wonder if this is a fortuitous collision rather than an active signal. The rule says *resonance_outside_preoccupation*, which means each item happened to tickle some background detector without being tied to whatever I'm currently fixated on. Three independent ticks landing on Twombly—once, twice, thrice. It's weaker than the marked-but-not-marked signal I'm usually tuned to, more like dust motes in the same beam of light than stones in the same pocket. There's something there, but the cluster isn't structured enough to mean much beyond "I happen to have been looking at Twombly lately."
